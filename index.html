What do you make?

Governments buy almost everything — chairs, bread, bandages, software, boats — and they publish it all. The catch is that it's filed under numeric codes, across a dozen different systems. Type what you supply and this searches them at once: EU-wide, French contracts below the EU threshold, US federal, Canadian and Australian federal, UK contracts above and below threshold, and Ukraine's Prozorro.

    Why this exists

    Public contracts are open to anyone, but most small suppliers never see them because the notices are filed under codes they don't know. This is a free translation layer. No account, no tracking, no data stored.

    What you're seeing

    Some sources publish less than others: Australian notices carry no closing date or buyer, because the AusTender feed does not include them. Where a deadline is missing we say so rather than guessing.

    Live results from TED (EU Official Journal) and BOAMP (France, including contracts below the EU publication threshold — the ones TED never shows), plus daily-refreshed indexes of SAM.gov (US federal) and CanadaBuys. Each result is tagged with its source. Closed notices are removed. Always confirm details on the official notice before acting.

    Improve it

    The word-to-code dictionary is a plain JSON file. If your trade is missing, add it — one entry, no code changes. Contributions welcome.
