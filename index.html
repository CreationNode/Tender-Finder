    Why this exists

    Public contracts are open to anyone, but most small suppliers never see them because the notices are filed under codes they don't know. This is a free translation layer. No account, no cookies, no ads.

    What we keep

    We count searches. When a search matches nothing in our dictionary, or finds no open tenders, we keep the words typed (cut to 60 characters, and dropped if they look like an email address or a long number) so we can add the missing trade. We keep nothing that identifies you: no IP address, no cookies, no browser details. Fonts are served from this site, and tender links take you straight to the official portal.

    Read before you bid

    Closed notices are removed and each result links to its official notice. Australian notices have no deadline in the source feed, so we show them as undated rather than guess. Brazilian tenders usually ask foreign bidders to register locally (SICAF) or appoint a representative. Always confirm details on the official notice.

    Improve it

    The word-to-code dictionary is one plain JSON file. If your trade is missing, add it or tell us. Contributions welcome. The code is MIT-licensed; each notice belongs to the portal that published it and is shown under that portal's terms (sources and terms).
